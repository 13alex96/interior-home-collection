About Us

Experience, design and personal service in Paphos.

Creating elegant interiors for more than 20 years

Interior Home Collection was founded by Panikos Panayi and Yiota Yennaris. With more than 20 years of experience in interior home decoration, we help clients select the right curtains, fabrics and finishing details for their homes and businesses.

Our work includes interior projects for hotels and apartments, including Capital Coast Resort, Paradise Resorts, Akamas Health Farm and many more.
